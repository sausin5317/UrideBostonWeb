Frequently Asked Questions

Clear information regarding our payout model, deductions, and Boston pilot policies.

How does the 100% earning transfer work? +

Uride is built with a driver-first philosophy. The fare paid by the rider for the trip is transferred directly to the driver once the ride is completed. Unlike legacy platforms, we do not take 40% to 50% arbitrary commission cuts.

What deductions apply to my earnings? +

To ensure full transparency: standard bank processing charges for electronic fund transfers and required commercial ride insurance are deducted. There are zero hidden platform markups—every deduction is itemized clearly on your trip summary.

When and where is the pilot launching? +

The initial pilot is limited to Greater Boston, Massachusetts (including Boston Core, Cambridge, Somerville, Allston/Brighton, and neighboring areas). Registered drivers will receive rolling invitations as pilot batches open.

Can I drive for Uride while also driving for other apps? +

Yes. You are completely independent. Participating in our Boston pilot runs does not prevent you from using other platforms.

What are the basic driver and vehicle requirements? +

A valid Massachusetts driver's license, an eligible 4-door vehicle in clean mechanical condition, and standard personal auto insurance. Further onboarding instructions will be shared prior to your first pilot run.
